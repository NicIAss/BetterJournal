# BetterJournal v0.1.0

BetterJournal replaces the journal list with a bookshelf, animated books and a separate tapestry gallery. It displays the vanilla and modded lore your player has recovered through the normal journal.

## Features

- Bookshelf with readable spines and recovered-fragment counts.
- Animated book opening, closing and page turns, including swipe controls.
- Fitted reading pages with adjustable text size.
- Bookmarks, NEW markers, saved reading positions and a Next unread button.
- Tapestry mosaics that fill in as intact pieces are found, with recovered lore alongside the artwork.
- Support for vanilla lore and modded entries delivered through the normal journal.
- Local progress with backup recovery and world/player separation.

## Install

For **Vintage Story 1.22.7**. Install **betterjournal-0.1.0.zip** on your client and press **J**, or your remapped journal key. The server does not need the mod.


## Progress and compatibility

You can install it in an existing world. Recovered book fragments are available immediately. Existing tapestry lore restores the matching standard artwork; individual pieces seen before installation without a lore unlock cannot be reconstructed and must be looked at again. Alternate artwork sharing lore, and designs without lore, also need direct observations. Looking at a rotten piece adds no new progress.

On a first installation without saved BetterJournal history, existing journal content shows NEW because vanilla does not supply reading history. Reading positions and individual tapestry observations are stored locally in `ModData/betterjournal/`, separately for each world and player.

Removing the ZIP restores the vanilla journal and leaves normal lore discoveries and world content intact. Local BetterJournal files remain. Reinstalling in the same profile restores unchanged reading history and remembered pieces; new journal lore starts unread. Pieces only looked at while the mod was absent are not tracked. Deleting the local files resets BetterJournal's extra history, not vanilla lore.

Other journal replacement mods and independent lore systems may require compatibility work. Removing a lore mod can leave journal records whose text or art needs that mod's assets. See [the full progress explanation](../PROGRESS.md).
