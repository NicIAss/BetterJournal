BetterJournal

By NiclAss · Client-side only · Vintage Story 1.22.7

BetterJournal replaces Vintage Story’s journal list with a bookshelf, animated books and a separate tapestry gallery. It displays the vanilla and modded lore your player has recovered through the normal journal.

Read your collected stories on fitted pages, adjust the text size, and return to unread passages using bookmarks and NEW markers. Tapestries have their own view for discovered artwork and recovered lore.

 Features
  • Collected journal entries become books with readable spines and recovered/total fragment counts, such as 5/8 for Letters.
  • Books have animated opening, closing and page turns. Long entries use fitted pages instead of scrolling, with adjustable text size.
  • Turn pages with buttons, arrow keys or a horizontal swipe.
  • NEW markers, bookmarks, saved positions and Next unread help you return to unfinished stories.
  • The tapestry gallery shows remembered artwork beside recovered lore, with missing pieces hidden.
  • Uses the game’s installed book textures, paper, window backgrounds and buttons.

Screenshots show an existing collection, including modded lore. Your collection depends on your own discoveries.

 Installation
  1. Install the BetterJournal ZIP in your game profile’s Mods folder. Keep it zipped.
  2. Start Vintage Story 1.22.7 and enter your world or server.
  3. Press J, or your remapped journal key.

Install BetterJournal on your client only. The server does not need the mod. You can add it to an existing singleplayer or multiplayer world; no new world or unexplored chunks are required.

Vanilla lore and modded entries delivered through the normal journal are supported, including BetterRuins. Independent lore systems, custom tapestry renderers and other journal replacements may need compatibility work. Other mods using J can compete for the same shortcut.

The tested game target is 1.22.7. Other game versions are not validated. First-time fitting of long text or a new text size can still cause a brief hitch.

 How it works
  1. When you install BetterJournal in an existing world, the book fragments already in your vanilla journal are available immediately. Fragments you have not found remain missing. The spine shows how many you found out of the full story; for example, five Letters fragments show 5/8.
  2. Existing entries initially show NEW if you have no saved BetterJournal reading history. Vanilla does not tell the mod which pages you already read.
  3. If you already recovered a tapestry’s lore, its matching standard design appears complete in the gallery with that lore. You do not need to find the original tapestry again.
  4. If you only looked at some tapestry pieces before installing the mod, look at those intact pieces again. Vanilla does not keep individual-piece viewing history. You also need to look again at designs that have no journal lore.
  5. If two tapestry designs share the same lore, that lore only reveals the standard design automatically. Look at the other design’s pieces to add its artwork to your collection.
  6. While the mod is installed, each intact piece you look at is remembered for that world and player. Pieces from different copies of the same design can fill the same picture. Looking at rotten pieces adds nothing; discoveries you already recorded stay remembered if the cloth later rots or disappears.
  7. Remembering every piece completes the gallery picture, but does not unlock its lore. The normal game still requires you to look at a complete, correctly assembled, intact tapestry in the world. A complete gallery picture can therefore have pending lore, and a design without assigned lore remains artwork only.
  8. A focused book page counts as viewed after it has settled for 1.5 seconds, or when you deliberately leave a fully displayed page. Animations and skipped pages do not count. Next unread takes you to text you have not viewed.
  9. A started book keeps its bookmark while story fragments are missing or the total is unknown. The bookmark disappears once the story is known to be complete and every recovered fragment has been viewed.
  10. A completed tapestry shows NEW until you view its completed detail screen. You do not have to visit every lore page. Partial designs do not show a completion prompt, and newly received or changed lore can make NEW appear again.
  11. If a mod inserts journal entries without defining the full story, BetterJournal cannot know how many fragments are still missing. Those entries show the number found followed by /? instead of claiming the story is complete. Entries defined in the vanilla or modded lore catalogue show the full total.
  12. Reading history and remembered pieces are saved in ModData/betterjournal/ on your computer, separately for each world and player. The mod does not rewrite the server journal, and this extra history does not automatically follow you to another computer or profile.
 Uninstalling

Remove the ZIP while the game is closed. On the next start, the vanilla journal returns. Normal lore discoveries, world blocks, items and existing tapestries remain intact. BetterJournal adds no world blocks or items.

Its local history files remain under ModData/betterjournal/. Reinstalling in the same profile restores unchanged reading history, bookmarks and remembered pieces for the same world/player.

Normal lore discoveries continue while the mod is absent. On reinstall, newly received journal fragments appear unread, and newly received tapestry lore can restore its standard artwork. Individual partial pieces only looked at during the absence are not tracked. Alternate and no-lore artwork still needs direct observations.

Deleting the local BetterJournal folder resets its extra history, not vanilla lore. Existing journal chapters remain available and can still restore matching standard tapestry artwork.

Removing a lore mod may leave saved journal entries whose text or art needs that mod’s assets. BetterJournal shows what the normal journal still contains; it does not supply missing assets. Removed entries leave dormant reading history. Restoring the same content and identity can restore its progress, while changed text, translations or IDs can appear unread again.
